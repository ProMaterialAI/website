Four org levels

Group → subsidiary → business line → department. 183 uses of "section", "office" and "sales org" were unified to department; 73 uses of "business unit" to business line.

gem_file.gem01 · oga15 / oea15 / pmm13 · abb05

Five inventory classes

Raw material / work in process / semi-finished / finished (in-house) / purchased goods. WIP ≠ semi-finished. Purchased goods are bought and resold unchanged at purchase cost; mixing them into finished goods distorts both turnover and margin. Everything in kg, scrap in tonnes.

Ratios are never stored

The DM layer stores only numerator and denominator; ratios are computed at query time as SUM(num)/SUM(den). Stored ratios break on roll-up: the average of department margins is not the company margin.

Price-adjusted revenue

Rare earths are priced at market, so raw-material moves pass straight into selling prices. Book revenue is +8.0% YoY; with pass-through removed it is +2.1%. Base, coefficient and formula are for the client to decide; candidate price feeds: Western Exchange, Baiinfo, Mysteel, Asian Metal.

FPY ≠ final yield

First-pass yield counts only units that pass every step without rework; final yield also counts reworked units that eventually pass. Plating rework hides inside final yield, so the Quality page reports FPY.

OTD has two clocks

On-time delivery is measured against the first promised date, not the latest re-promised one. Measuring against the latest date makes every order "on time", the same trap as supplier lead time (pmn33 vs pmn36).

Reporting currency: US dollars

The ERP books in CNY; the report shows US$. P&L lines convert at the monthly average rate, balance-sheet items (cash, receivables, inventory) at the month-end rate, the usual translation method under IAS 21.

★★ Rate source (bank, PBOC central parity or treasury rate) and who maintains it are for the client to decide. Exchange-rate moves show up as a separate translation difference, not as operating performance.

Blockers

Three SQL queries; each one unblocks a group of reports

Which field carries "business line"

TIPTOP has no field called business line. Candidates: the 10 flexible analysis codes on abb_file / profit centre gem09='2' / product family.

SELECT abb11, abb12, abb13, abb14,
       COUNT(*)
FROM   abb_file
GROUP BY abb11, abb12, abb13, abb14;

Blocks: margin drill-down, revenue mix, expenses, receivables, monthly overview. Reps cannot be attached under a business line, so the chain breaks at level 3.

How to split the five inventory classes

imd10 warehouse class only has S / W, and the dictionary says S is a general warehouse holding raw materials, semi-finished and finished goods. Stock is mixed, so classification must come from the item side in two steps.

SELECT imz106, imz109, imz131,
       COUNT(*)
FROM   imz_file
GROUP BY imz106, imz109, imz131;

Sales rep is not on the documents

oea_file and oga_file carry only the department code; the rep comes via the customer, occ_file.occ04. One customer, one rep; occ04 holds only the current value, so when a rep changes, last year's margin moves to the new person. The warehouse needs SCD Type 2.

Machine time is not in the ERP

OEE needs run time, stop time and ideal cycle time per machine. TIPTOP holds work orders (sfb_file) but not machine states. Source must be MES, PLC counters or a shift log; decide before the Scheduling page can go live.
