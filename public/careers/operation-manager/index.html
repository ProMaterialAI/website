Operation Manager

Work closely with our founder across communications, programme coordination and company operations.

Part-time Port Talbot / Hybrid Approximately 2 days per week

About ProMaterial

ProMaterial is an AI-driven advanced manufacturing company developing intelligent inspection and machining technologies for high-performance materials used in electric vehicles, renewable energy, robotics and industrial manufacturing.

Operating across the UK and Hong Kong, ProMaterial is supported by Innovate UK, the Welsh Government and Women TechEU.

The role

We are looking for a part-time Operation Manager to work closely with the founder across the following areas:

  • PR and Communications: Developing company content, communicating key milestones, supporting external visibility and identifying relevant media, award and speaking opportunities.
  • Programme and Project Coordination: Coordinating grant-funded and international projects, tracking milestones and deliverables, supporting reporting, and ensuring agreed actions are followed through.
  • Other Operational Work.

About you

  • Organised, thoughtful and detail-oriented, with strong written communication and research skills.
  • Comfortable managing several workstreams independently and taking ownership of agreed outcomes.
  • Experience in communications, marketing, research, programme delivery or project coordination would be valuable.
  • A background in AI or manufacturing is preferred, together with an interest in technology, innovation and entrepreneurship.

What we offer

  • Direct exposure to building and scaling an international technology company.
  • High-quality training and development opportunities through programmes and networks including Barclays Eagle Labs and other innovation partners.
  • Engagement with technology, investment and entrepreneurship ecosystems across the UK and Hong Kong.
