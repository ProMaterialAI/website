Gender Equality Plan (GEP)

Owner: ProMaterial Ltd. We integrate gender equality into strategy, operations, and research activity.

Introduction

ProMaterial is committed to building an inclusive workplace that values diversity and supports equal opportunity at every level of the organization.

Strategic objectives and actions

  1. Work-life balance and organizational culture: implement flexible working arrangements, remote options, and inclusive support networks.
  2. Gender balance in leadership and decision-making: increase representation of women in leadership and improve transparency in decisions.
  3. Equality in recruitment and progression: use gender-neutral job language, structured interviews, and fair promotion criteria.
  4. Gender dimension in research content: include gender perspectives in project design, methods, and reporting where relevant.

Monitoring and reporting

  • Track implementation through periodic internal review.
  • Publish progress updates to maintain transparency.
  • Refine actions based on evidence and stakeholder feedback.

Public accessibility

This Gender Equality Plan is publicly accessible on the ProMaterial website and intended for continuous improvement with community feedback.
